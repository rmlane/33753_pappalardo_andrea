i'm planning statistical analysis of a crossover stepped wedge trial. the unit of observation is schools. different schools started the program in different school years. they also started at different points in the schoolyear so have different levels of exposure in the first year. i have pre-enrollment baseline data for all schools. the outcome of interest is change in a numeric variable (call it x_change). how to structure a marginal GEE model to capture the effects of calendar time/school year, (possibly fractional) time in the program, enrollment order, and baseline level of x? assume schools were randomized to 4 groups with staggered start times.

Copilot said:
For a stepped wedge crossover design with school-level observations and repeated annual measurements, a marginal GEE framework is a reasonable choice. The key issue is disentangling:

Secular calendar time effects (school year trends).
Intervention exposure effects (including partial first-year exposure).
Randomization sequence effects (enrollment order/wave).
Baseline imbalance in the outcome.
Within-school correlation across repeated observations.
